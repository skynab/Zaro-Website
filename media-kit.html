Descriptions

Ready-to-paste copy. Edit it to fit, but please keep the facts as they are.

Zaro

Zaro Software LLC makes free, open-source desktop apps for creative work. Its apps are CutReel, a non-linear video editor, and SoundSplice, a multi-track audio editor. Both run on Windows, macOS and Linux.

CutReel

CutReel is a free, open-source non-linear video editor from Zaro. You can edit, colour grade, mix and deliver in one window, using four workspaces that share one timeline. It's written in C++20 on Qt 6 and FFmpeg, composites on the GPU, and runs on Windows, macOS and Linux.

SoundSplice

SoundSplice is a free, open-source multi-track audio editor from Zaro. You can record, import, edit and process audio on a timeline, mix with per-track effects, and export a full mix or stems. It runs on Windows, macOS and Linux.

Guidelines

Please do

  • Use the files as they come, at any size
  • Leave clear space around a logo at least as wide as the mark's corner radius
  • Link to the app's page or to the Downloads page
  • Write the names as Zaro, CutReel and SoundSplice

Please don't

  • Stretch, rotate, recolour or add effects to the logos or icons
  • Put the white wordmark on a light background, or the dark one on a dark background
  • Suggest that Zaro endorses your product or content
  • Use the logos as part of your own product's name or logo
