Core interactions

Editing that stays out of your way

The everyday tools are right on the timeline, and every edit can be undone.

Clips on a timeline

Move, resize, copy and duplicate clips, or drag them between tracks on a zoomable timeline.

Record

Record from any audio input, with count-in, a metronome and input monitoring.

Waveform editor

Cut, trim, split, fade, reverse, normalise and change speed or pitch, down to the sample.

Noise reduction

Capture a noise print from a quiet passage, then remove it from the rest of the clip.

Automation

Draw or record gain and pan automation on each track, and on the master.

Workspace

Pick a layout or build your own

Panes dock wherever you drag their tabs. Start from the built-in layouts and arrange them however you like.

Effects

An effect chain on every track

Each track has an insert chain of built-in effects and hosted plugins. The master bus has the same chain.

FilterEQDelayReverbDrive CompressorGateTremoloChorusWobble VST3 pluginsAU plugins
Mix and master

Finish the mix in the same window

A mixer strip for each track, and a mastering rack on the master bus.

  • Gain, pan, mute, solo and a meter on every strip
  • Bus tracks that mix whatever is routed or sent to them
  • Mastering rack: EQ, exciter, widener, reverb and maximiser, with presets
  • A spectrum analyser for any selection
Export

A full mix, or every track on its own

Export the whole mix, or write one stem per track.

Under the hood

Framework

C++20 on JUCE 8

Audio I/O, plugin hosting and the interface come from one native codebase.

Plugins

VST3 + AU

Host third-party plugins in the same chain as the built-in effects.

Projects

.splice files

Projects save as .splice files, with undo and redo for every edit.
