FAQ

Are the apps really free?

Yes. CutReel and SoundSplice are free to download and use, including commercially. There's no subscription, account or watermark.

Which operating systems are supported?

Both apps run on Windows, macOS and Linux. The Downloads page lists the package for each one.

Are they open source?

Yes. CutReel's source is Apache-2.0, and its distributed binaries are GPL-3.0 because of the libraries they include. SoundSplice's source is MIT. See Licenses.

Can CutReel open Premiere Pro or Final Cut projects?

CutReel reads and writes OpenTimelineIO, FCP7 XML (used by Premiere Pro) and FCPXML (used by Final Cut Pro), so cuts can move in both directions.

Which audio formats can SoundSplice export?

WAV, AIFF, FLAC, Ogg Vorbis and MP3, as a full mix or as one stem per track.

Where do I report a bug or request a feature?

Open an issue on the app's GitHub repository. The links are under Community and support below.
